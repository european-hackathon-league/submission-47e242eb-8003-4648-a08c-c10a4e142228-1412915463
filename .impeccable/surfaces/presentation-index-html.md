# Surface: pitch demo deck (presentation/index.html)

Mode: Persuade. 3-minute hard-cut pitch to BMW PM judges, presenter clicking through a simulated product demo, then 3 min Q&A.

Audience/job: BMW PMs must believe the tool fits their own process and is honest. Proof: real G60 counts (5,005 findings; US 4,365; touch screen 26/72 "Difficult to Use"; seats 46/105 complaints/defects; exterior design 144/157 likes; handling 215/227 likes). Constraint: low click count; customer value, not specs.

Unresolved: target market shown is United States because G60 feedback has no China rows (team originally mocked China).

## Direction contract

THESIS: The deck is the next chapter of BMW's own PM keynote: their "The Process" slide turned into working software. Refuses the generic SaaS dark-dashboard with rounded glowing cards.

OWN-WORLD: Keynote ground (navy to petrol to plum atmospheric gradient, the keynote's own image), white 1.5px hairline rectangles at 0 radius, bold condensed uppercase headlines ending in a period, Light condensed body, ice/petrol tints (#C3E7F0, #85ACB9, #548D9E), BMW blue #1c69d4 only on the primary action, M red only for rejection. Robot and person glyphs from the keynote mark who authored each item: AI proposes, PM decides.

STORY: PMs see their process, see the AI rank with evidence, watch it argue against itself, reject and recalibrate, capture the why by voice, and end at one traceable car.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Title slide: headline "BMW PRODUCT STRATEGY AI." huge condensed left, light subline beneath, the keynote's camouflaged concept car bleeding off the right edge, meta line bottom-left. Demo slides: app frame of hairline boxes with a persistent process rail along the bottom (Situation analysis › Requirements › Prioritization › Documentation › One car) with an arrow that advances.

FORM: Keynote-continuation (brief-pinned by the user; no concept-seed roll; position 1).

SIGNATURE: the process-rail arrow advancing between auto-animated slides; requirement rows physically re-ordering on rejection.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
